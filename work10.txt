199. Explain BODMAS in Python
BODMAS describes the order of mathematical operations:

* **B** — Brackets `()`
* **O** — Orders/exponents `**`
* **D** — Division `/`
* **M** — Multiplication `*`
* **A** — Addition `+`
* **S** — Subtraction `-`

Python also follows precedence rules where multiplication/division are done before addition/subtraction.
